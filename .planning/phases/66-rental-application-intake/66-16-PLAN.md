<objective>
Wire approve → convert: teach `AddTenantForm` to accept initial values, forward them from
**both** call sites, prefill from the application row, and record the conversion when the

Purpose: APPLY-04 — approving an application converts the applicant into a tenant record
with prefilled fields.

Output: one prop, two call-site edits, one notice component, two test files.

## Prefill exactly five fields, and do not widen the tenants schema

`tenants` carries `first_name`, `last_name`, `name`, `email`, `phone`, `date_of_birth`,
`emergency_contact_*`, `identity_verified`, `ssn_last_four`, `status`, `owner_user_id`.
`AddTenantForm` submits only `email`, `first_name`, `last_name`, `name`, `phone`. The
application collects far more — address, income, references — and **none of it has a home
on `tenants`**.

So prefill those five and stop. Do NOT widen the tenants schema to absorb application
fields: it would duplicate data, contradict "tenants are records", and — decisively —
create a **second retention surface for applicant PII outside the sweep**. The remaining
detail stays on the application row and is reachable from the tenant through the
`converted_tenant_id` reverse lookup.
</objective>
