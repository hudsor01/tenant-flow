## Why one scroll and not a stepper

Recorded here because a reviewer will suggest a stepper for a 26-field form, and the
answer is not taste:

1. **State loss is unrecoverable on this surface.** The applicant has no account and D-10
   forbids emailing them anything, so there is no resume link and no draft. Every
   navigation before the single commit point is another chance to lose 26 fields with no
   recovery path. A stepper's Back gesture on mobile IS the browser's Back gesture, and
   `NuqsAdapter` is scoped to `(owner)` and `blog`, so a `?step=` param is not available
   here.
2. **Server validation errors can land on an unrendered step.** The Edge Function's
   validator is authoritative; a step-1 error cannot be shown while step 4 is mounted.
3. **Autofill.** Mobile browsers fill name/email/phone/street/city/state/ZIP in one pass
   when the fields are in one document. Splitting them across mounting steps breaks the
   heuristic and costs more taps than the chunking saves.
4. **Honesty.** On a 42 U.S.C. § 3604(c)-regulated surface, concealing length to maximise
   submit rate is the wrong instinct.
</objective>
