# Phase 66.2 Plan 34: MainNav on the canonical sidebar Summary

MainNav now renders on the shadcn sidebar primitives:
- SidebarMenu rows as `asChild` Links with `isActive` and `aria-current`;
- two controlled Radix Collapsible sections with SidebarMenuSub children;
- a two-link SidebarFooter (Settings, Help & Support).

Section state is lifted into `AppShellFrame` through the new `useNavSectionState` hook. The section holding the active route opens, navigating into a section opens it, and a manual toggle survives the mobile sheet unmounting.
