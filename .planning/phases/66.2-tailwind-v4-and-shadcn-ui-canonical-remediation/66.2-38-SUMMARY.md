## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 1 - evidence quality] Frame-strip method.** Pausing and stepping every animation (the literal method, kept as mode `literal`) also froze the dialog's exit, the KPI tickers and the charts, so the frames showed a dialog and zero KPI values. Added mode `isolated`: freeze the timeline, pause only the BlurFade animations, finish every other finite animation, restore the rate, wait 2.5 s, then step BlurFade. Both modes are recorded and both end at opacity 1. The timeline is frozen with CDP `Animation.setPlaybackRate(0)` before navigation (each entrance otherwise finishes before the page settles). On `/` the page is scrolled (instant, overriding the global `scroll-behavior: smooth`) to the first sized wrapper.
2. **Credential gate:** `test -f .env.local` (execution mode), not the plan's `printenv` loop; the script checks presence in-process and prints nothing.
3. **Task 1 committed on its own** (`def0bf229`) at the orchestrator's instruction; the plan had Task 4 commit the record. The Task 4 record commit is `cdcffc1e1`.
