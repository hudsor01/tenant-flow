# Phase 66.2: Plan Outline

**Shape:** 39 plans, one branch, one PR (D-01). Each plan is its own execution wave, and the waves form a strict chain.
- Every plan depends on the plan before it. A phase-wave's **gate plan** (10, 15, 17, 19, 29, 37) runs the full PLAN.md §7 protocol (D-43) and is the barrier the next phase-wave's first plan depends on.
- **Why the chain is strictly sequential:** almost every plan edits `src/app/globals.css` or `src/app/__tests__/tailwind-theme-integrity.test.ts`. All plans share one worktree, and lefthook runs the whole-repo lint, typecheck and unit + coverage suite on every commit. Parallel executors would collide on files and on the git index, and a half-finished sibling would turn their commits red.
- **Wave column:** the GSD execution wave number. The `[Wn]` tag in each Objective is the phase-wave from CONTEXT (W0 = measurement prep, F = final gate).
- **Pull-forwards** (RESEARCH H1–H3, so that every guard lands green with its fix):
  - the brand `--color-sidebar-*` `@theme` block (D-21) and the bogus `@source` line (B9 part) move into W1;
  - LoadingSpinner C12d moves into W2.
- **Every plan carries a `<threat_model>`** (ASVS L1, block on high). Threat T-66.2-SC appears in plan 02 (package declaration), T-CSP in 05, 24, 26 and 37, and T-COOKIE in 31, 33 and 36.
